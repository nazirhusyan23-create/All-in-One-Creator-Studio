Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how All-in-One Creator ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our website and browser-based tools. We built this site around a simple principle: your media files should never have to leave your device just to be edited. Please read this policy to understand what limited data we do collect, and how it is used.

1. Files You Process On This Site

The photo, video, and PDF tools, along with the text and QR utilities, run entirely client-side in your browser using JavaScript APIs such as Canvas, MediaRecorder, and the File API. This means images, videos, documents, and text you use with these tools are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded to, transmitted to, or stored on our servers. We have no access to and cannot see, copy, or retain any file you process using our tools.

2. Information We Do Collect

Like most websites, we may automatically collect limited, non-identifying technical information when you visit, including:

  • Browser type and version, device type, and operating system
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (country/region level)
  • Pages visited, time spent on the site, and referring website
  • Standard server log data such as timestamps and IP address

If you contact us by email, we collect the information you choose to include in that message, such as your email address and the content of your inquiry, solely to respond to you.

3. Cookies and Advertising

This site is supported by display advertising. We and our advertising partners, including Google AdSense, may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to serve ads, measure ad performance, and limit how many times you see an ad.

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendor use of cookies at aboutads.info.

4. Third-Party Services

Our tools rely on a small number of third-party JavaScript libraries loaded from public content delivery networks (for example, pdf-lib, JSZip, and a QR code library) to power specific in-browser features. These libraries execute inside your browser and do not transmit your files to us or to the library provider. We may also use third-party analytics services to understand aggregate site usage; these services operate under their own privacy policies.

5. Data Retention

Because files processed by our tools never reach our servers, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on your behalf. Technical log data and any email correspondence are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for security, support, and legal compliance purposes.

6. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact Us page.
