About Us

All-in-One Creator is a free, browser-based hub of media utilities and creator revenue calculators, built for content creators, freelancers, students, and small business owners who need quick tools without the friction of installing software, creating an account, or uploading sensitive files to a stranger's server. Every tool on this site — including the background eraser, batch image compressor, video trimmer, resolution resizer, and PDF merge/split/compress utilities — runs entirely on your own device using native browser technologies such as the Canvas API, the MediaRecorder API, and the open-source pdf-lib and JSZip libraries.

We started this project after noticing how many "free online tools" quietly upload your photos, videos, or documents to a remote server just to perform a simple edit. That approach is slow, and it means your files are, at least briefly, sitting on infrastructure you don't control. All-in-One Creator takes the opposite approach: processing happens client-side, in your browser, so your media never leaves your computer or phone.

Our Mission

Our mission is to give creators worldwide fast, reliable, and privacy-respecting tools for everyday media and document tasks, alongside honest, transparent calculators for estimating creator revenue — without paywalls, mandatory sign-ups, or hidden data collection. We believe the tools people use to manage their creative work should be as accessible as the platforms they publish on.

What You'll Find on This Site

  • Photo Tools — a background eraser that removes flat or gradient backgrounds using an edge flood-fill algorithm, and a batch image compressor for shrinking JPEG, PNG, and WebP files for faster-loading pages.
  • Video Tools — a trimmer for cutting promotional clips and short-form previews, and a resolution resizer that converts landscape footage into vertical 9:16 formats for Reels, Shorts, and TikTok.
  • PDF Tools — utilities to merge multiple PDFs into one document, split a PDF into a page-per-file ZIP archive, and compress PDFs by stripping unused metadata.
  • Revenue Calculators — a set of ten calculators covering ad revenue, sponsorship rates, freelance pricing, and other creator-economy earnings estimates, built with transparent formulas so you can see exactly how each figure is derived.

How We Handle Your Data

Because every tool runs client-side, we do not receive, store, or have access to the images, videos, or documents you process on this site. Calculator inputs (such as impression counts or CPM figures) are used only to compute the result shown to you in your browser session and are not saved to a server or shared with third parties. Like most free websites, this site is supported by display advertising served by third-party ad networks, which may use cookies in line with standard advertising practices.
